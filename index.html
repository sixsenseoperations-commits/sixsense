FAQ

What is Six Sense?

A creator-led performance marketing network built around authentic short-form video. We use creator content to test and improve ad creatives for digital campaigns.

Is this an influencer program?

No. You’re part of an early-stage growth system where your content helps us learn what resonates with real audiences and shape how products are presented to the market.

What do I need to film?

A natural, relatable video about the product or service in your brief. Vertical (9:16), 20–40 seconds, shot on a smartphone with clear audio and good lighting. Introduce the product, share your experience, highlight one or two benefits, and end with a simple call to action if your brief includes one.

What can’t I say in my video?

No income claims or earning promises, no guaranteed results, no false or misleading statements, no copyrighted music, and no offensive language.

What if I don’t know what to say?

Your brief includes content angles to pick from, such as a first impression, a problem and solution, a daily routine, or a casual recommendation to a friend.

How long do I have to submit?

48 hours after you receive your brief. Include your name and creator ID, if assigned.

How and when do I get paid?

Through GCash. Once your video is reviewed and approved, payment is processed within 24 hours and you receive a confirmation message. Rates are shared with you after approval and may vary by campaign performance and creator tier.

What if I’m asked to revise my video?

We’ll send clear, specific feedback. You’re expected to complete reasonable revisions within the agreed timeframe. Videos that stray far from the brief may be rejected.

How will my video be used?

By submitting a video, you give Six Sense the right to use, edit, adapt, and distribute it across our marketing channels, including paid social ads, organic posts, our website, email, and other digital campaigns. This right is perpetual, worldwide, and royalty-free unless agreed otherwise in writing.
